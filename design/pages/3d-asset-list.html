For the three.js build

3D asset list

Look: chunky low-poly, toon shading with a 3-step ramp, navy #002244 inverted-hull outline (~3px), flat palette colors, no textures. Budget: one GLB per scene, Draco-compressed, under 2 MB total. Motion: every rig ships a still pose for reduced motion.

01 · Cartoon plane

P1
Loader · Hero · Journey · Globe (mini)
Rig: body squash/stretch, wobbling tail fin, spinning nose cap. Bobs ±6px idle, banks into turns, loop-the-loop on click.

02 · Tow banner + rope

P1
Hero
6-bone cloth strip that ripples in the wind; flutters faster on hover. Text baked as a swappable texture-free mesh.

03 · Smiling sun

P1
Hero
Shape keys: blink, squint, grin. Rays rotate as one ring. Rises with a squash; click sets it behind the dunes (dark mode).

04 · Sleepy moon

P1
Hero (dark theme)
Shape keys: sleepy blink, smile. Rises when the sun sets, stars fade in around it; click for sunrise.

05 · Beach ball

P1
Hero · Playground
Physics sphere (one shared instance). Grab, throw, bounce; 0.8 squash on impact; floats with buoyancy in the pool.

06 · Puffy clouds ×3

P1
Everywhere
Instanced, three silhouettes. Parallax at 0.2 / 0.4 / 0.6; puff up 5% when the plane passes through.

07 · Sea + sand ground

P1
Hero
Two toon planes; sea gets a scrolling stripe shader for the waves. Sand dunes as one low-poly mesh. Night palette in dark mode.

08 · Palm tree

P2
Hero
Bone chain trunk, five leaf bones. Sways with cursor velocity; drops a coconut after 10 fast shakes.

09 · Suitcase ×2

P2
Hero · Journey 03
Hinged lid: pops open on hover with squash, snaps shut on the Journey step. Sun and sky color variants.

10 · Folded map + pin

P2
Journey 01
Map unfolds in 3 panels; pin drops from above and wobbles in.

11 · Ticket + stamp

P2
Journey 02
Ticket slides in, stamp slams down with a little camera shake and a green check decal.

12 · Globe + pins + arcs

P2
Where we go
Drag to spin with inertia. Arcs are tube meshes drawn in by progress; pins bounce on hover.

13 · Pool + water

P2
Playground
Rounded tile rim; toon water with ripple rings from the ball. Splash particles on a miss.

14 · Pool ring

P3
Playground
Floats and drifts; the ball can land in it for a bonus point.

15 · Volleyball net

P3

Two poles + cloth net that dents when the ball hits it.

16 · Cartoon crab

P3

17 · Sunglasses
